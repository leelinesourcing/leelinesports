# Brand Brief — LeelineSports

## Positioning
- **Business:** Custom sportswear manufacturer + end-to-end B2B supply-chain partner (OEM/ODM, QC, DDP/FBA logistics) bridging Chinese manufacturing and Western e-commerce.
- **Ideal Customer:** B2B — private-label brands, Amazon FBA sellers, gyms, teams/leagues, and established retailers.
- **Primary CTA:** Request a quote / project brief (form: name, company, email, phone, product type, MOQ/order size, target date).
- **Differentiator:** Risk-mitigated scaling — flexible OEM/ODM at low MOQ (100–300 pcs), AQL 2.5 QC, DDP delivered-duty-paid pricing, 5,000+ vetted suppliers, Wuhan ground presence, FBA-native logistics.
- **Positioning Statement:** "Bring us a sketch or a tech pack — we handle sourcing, quality control, and logistics so you can scale without touching a shipment."

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Personality:** Assured, technical, protective (Guardian archetype) with a hint of craft/transformation (Magician).
- **Tone:** "We handle the mess, you handle the sales." Calm, evidence-forward, zero hype.
- **We are:** the outsourced procurement department for growing brands.
- **We are NOT:** a commodity factory, a trading-company middleman, or a discount Alibaba vendor.

## Visual Identity

### Color Constraints
- **Keep:** the LeelineSports logo (use a white/knockout variant on dark heroes).
- **Move away from:** the olive-green `#5B7F1D` / dark-green `#273227` identity.
- Final palette + fonts are selected per concept direction (see `directions.json`).

### Visual Style
- **Keywords:** premium, athletic, trustworthy, modern, evidence-driven
- **Do:** bold display type, full-bleed athletic/craft photography, strong single accent, real numbers/specs everywhere.
- **Don't:** generic SaaS cards, all-caps eyebrow labels everywhere, cream+terracotta clichés, vague adjectives ("high quality", "best service", "reliable partner").

## The 5 ICPs (copy targeting + "who this is for")
1. **FBA Front-Runner** — solo Amazon private-label seller; fears TOS compliance, see-through fabric, customs surprises.
2. **Gym Visionary** — gym/CrossFit owner; wants high-margin branded merch, sizes XS–3XL, sublimated (not heat-pressed) logos.
3. **Influencer Icon** — creator launching a merch line; needs exact Pantone matching, NDA, ethical/recycled fabrics.
4. **League Logician** — procurement manager for teams/schools; deadline-driven, values consistency + low-MOQ reorders.
5. **Diversifying Retailer** — $1M+ e-commerce manager; wants supply-chain redundancy + honest QC reports.

## Evidence to weave into copy (real numbers from the business)
- AQL 2.5 (major defects) / AQL 4.0 (minor defects)
- ISO 5077 (dimensional stability, shrinkage ≤3–5%), ISO 105 (colorfastness, Grade 4+)
- MOQ: 50–100 stock/blanks, 200–300 custom private label
- 5,000+ vetted suppliers; Wuhan consolidated warehousing + kitting
- DDP pricing (manufacturing + shipping + duties — no surprise fees)
- Sampling: 5–10 days, refundable fees on bulk order
- Logistics: Express 3–5d / Air 7–12d / Sea 25–40d
- FBA prep: barcoding, poly-bagging, direct injection into Amazon FCs
- Live video inspections + "man-in-plant" supervision
